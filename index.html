Ritmo

A practice partner for salsa dancers. Build a routine, set the tempo, and let the count, music, and voice guide you.

Practice your routine
in ritmo.

Sequence your moves, dial the tempo, and dance through the routine with voice prompts and percussion that keep you on the beat — whether you're learning Salsa On 2 or working through a Bachata set.

  • Build named routines from a library of moves
  • Adjustable tempo from slow practice to performance speed
  • Toggle voice prompts, music, drums, and count independently
  • Salsa On 2 and Bachata supported at launch

Privacy policy

The short version: we don't want your data.

This policy applies to Coconut Donkey and all of our apps, including Ritmo. We keep it short on purpose.

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Coconut Donkey ("we", "us") makes iPhone apps. We respect your privacy and try to collect as little personal information as possible. This page explains what we do and don't collect, and how we handle it.

What we collect

Our apps store your data (such as the routines and moves you create in Ritmo) locally on your device. We do not upload that content to our servers, and we cannot read it.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use that only to reply.

What we don't collect

  • We don't have user accounts.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't use third-party analytics SDKs.

Anonymous crash reports

With your permission, Apple may share aggregated, anonymized crash and performance reports with us through standard App Store mechanisms. These help us fix bugs and contain no personally identifying information. You can opt out at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of our apps after a change means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@coconutdonkey.com.
